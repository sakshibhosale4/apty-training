Chicken Pizza

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp dry yeast
  • ¾ cup warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup cooked chicken breast (shredded)
  • ½ cup sliced bell peppers
  • ½ cup sliced onions
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Chili flakes (optional)

Preparation Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix yeast, sugar, and warm water. Let it sit for 10 minutes until frothy.
  2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Knead into a soft dough. Cover and let rise for 1 hour.
  3. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C). Roll out the dough into a pizza base.
  4. Spread pizza sauce evenly, followed by shredded chicken, bell peppers, and onions.
  5. Top with mozzarella cheese, oregano, and chili flakes if desired.
  6. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is melted and bubbly.
  7. Slice and serve hot with your favorite dip or soda!

Final Dish

A cheesy, flavorful chicken pizza fresh out of the oven, ready to enjoy!
